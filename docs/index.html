Pomodoro Studio

Pomodoro Studio is a personal, self-hosted application that creates original long-form Pomodoro focus videos (study timers with ambient sounds and calm music) and publishes them to the The Amber Window YouTube channel.

How it works

  1. The channel owner chooses a theme, an environment (rain, fireplace, forest…) and a duration.
  2. The app runs on the owner's own computer, creates an original background image, mixes licensed ambient sounds and music, and renders the video with an on-screen Pomodoro timer.
  3. With the owner's explicit authorisation, it uploads the finished video and its thumbnail to the owner's own YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API.

Use of Google and YouTube data

The app only requests permission to upload videos (youtube.upload). It is used exclusively by the channel owner to publish videos to their own channel. It does not read, collect, store or share data about any other user. See the Privacy Policy.
